What behavior landed?

five contracts

ResolvedScoring

  • Aggregation now requires the planned repeat identities, materializes absent task/repeat cells, and rejects duplicate or unexpected cells.
  • Task and stratum children are labeled. A task with multiple canonical strata contributes its complete repeat aggregate once to each named stratum.

ResolvedPool splitting

  • Splits group by the complete strata tuple and draw combinations round-robin in first-seen order.
  • Within-combination order, exact role sizes, disjointness, and allowed unassigned tails remain explicit.

ResolvedManifest boundaries

  • Persisted manifests require the exact five-field current schema and reject coercible, malformed, unknown, or future-version data.
  • All construction paths share one invariant validator; stratum counts are detached into a read-only mapping.

ResolvedInstance immutability

  • Instances always snapshot prompt inputs, validate string keys and values, and remain hash-stable after caller mutation.
  • Direct strata must be a tuple; blank labels are rejected; repeated labels are deduplicated in first-seen order.

ResolvedProbe behavior

  • Normalization removes complete nested fence wrappers to a fixed point while preserving unmatched fences and internal backticks.
  • ProbePair now supplies its documented prompt-input-only renderer by default.
